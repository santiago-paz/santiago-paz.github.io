# Home page and site shell

## Scope and mode
Whole-site replacement of the visual world, led by the home page (`app/page.tsx`). The same world reaches the shared layout, About, Writing, posts, project pages, 404 and the share cards. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job and action
Recruiters screen first: role, stack, location, work permit, languages, CV and email within ten seconds. Engineering managers judge second: proof that each product was built alone and runs live. Primary action: Download CV (PDF). Second action: email. The CV stays reachable from the header on every page.

## Proof and content
Real public first screens of Contract Lens, Multi-Agent Trading Desk and Reema, captured from their live sites; nothing behind a login or "Enter Desktop". bedrock-genai-labs shows its real five exam domains from its README instead of a picture. Each project carries a short "built" list taken from its own role text. The headshot is approved for the page and the share cards.

## Constraints
Static export on GitHub Pages. WCAG 2.2 AA. Plain hyphens in all English copy. Project order stays the CV order. Routes, JSON-LD, llms.txt, RSS and the SP icon set stay.

## Memorable moment
The strike: every hallmark row lands mark by mark, on load in the hero and as each plate enters view.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One maker, whole products. Every project is a piece struck with the same SP maker's mark and a row of hallmarks anyone can check live. It refuses the category default: a name hero over a grid of same-size project cards, and the text-left, image-right feature bands of a SaaS landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Polished steel ground #EDF0F3 with ink #111418; blued steel #2A44A8 is the maker's color. Flat beveled cartouche marks in Mozilla Headline capitals: struck solid when built, pale when not yet built. Body in Mozilla Text. Each live product owns a full-bleed plate in its own real colors: Contract Lens white with seal red #A3202F, Trading Desk desktop teal #008080 with Win98 grey #C0C0C0, Reema night #00131F with aqua #25FFCD. Real product screens are the only pictures. No textures, no hairline grids, no eyebrows, no section numbers.

STORY: A recruiter reads the name, the role and the register of struck facts, then downloads the CV. An engineering manager meets the first plate struck SP and LIVE, opens the product, and scrolls through plates that each list what was built, from the data model up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, Santiago Paz in Mozilla Headline at 88 to 96px, then the role line, the tagline, and Download CV as the blued primary plate with Email beside it. Right, the register: five struck marks with plain facts (solo work, Berlin and remote, EU work permit, 13 years and stack, languages), with the 4:5 portrait set top right as on a German CV. The white Contract Lens plate begins at the fold with its name and hallmark row. Signature interaction: the strike, each mark pressed with a flash of its own ink for about half a second, 90ms apart, fully visible without motion.

FORM: The Maker's Mark (assay hallmarks), sixth of seven grounded candidates, seed 07665578. Raised by Football Kit Wall (each product wears its own colorway, the maker's mark never moves), Fashion Flagship (real product screens are the only imagery) and Coiled Earth Tower (each plate lists its layers in build order).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
